01 / Our Operating Mandate

Do not sell decoration. Fix the everyday friction people put up with.

Across Indian retail markets and basic household utilities, buyers routinely face short piece counts in wholesale stacks, flour fillers disguised as durum pasta, brooms whose glued caps snap off on week two, rental brokers demanding unearned commissions, and credit reports locked inside complex English. Eshanura builds physical and software fixes from the ground up.

Rule 01 · Fair Count & Weight

Zero hidden margin cuts

Whether it is a 25-plate + 50-bowl Bajrangi Annapurna stack or a 100 g California almond pouch, every unit leaves our floor at exact declared count and weight.

Rule 02 · Structural Utility

Built into the mould, never glued

An EasyCare hanging loop is injection-moulded as one body with the handle so brooms hang upright off wet floors for the entire life of the bristles.

Rule 03 · 3-Second Shelf Read

Readable at 30 cm and 80 px

On crowded kirana racks and quick-commerce phone grids alike, every pack answers Category, Variant, and Proof in under 3 seconds.

Rule 04 · Plain Indian Speech

Zero legal or credit fog

Dorway QR posters work on any phone camera at the house gate. CLYR translates confusing bureau flags into plain Hindi and English actions.
